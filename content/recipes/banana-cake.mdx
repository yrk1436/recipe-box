      - "1 1/2 cups wheat flour"

steps:
  - "Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a loaf pan."
  - "Blend the sugar, softened butter, and eggs together with a beater."
  - "In a bowl, mix the wheat flour, baking soda, baking powder, and chopped nuts."
  - "Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, then add the mashed bananas and vanilla. Mix until combined."
  - "Pour into the greased loaf pan. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes."

tips:
  - "Use very ripe bananas for the best flavor and sweetness."
  - "Soften the butter before beating so it blends smoothly with the sugar and eggs."

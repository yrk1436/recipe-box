ingredients:
  - section: "For the Marinade"
    items:
      - "500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces"
      - "1 cup plain yogurt"
      - "1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste"
      - "1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder"
      - "1 tsp garam masala"
      - "Salt to taste"
  - section: "For the Sauce"
    items:
      - "3 tbsp butter"
      - "1 tbsp oil"
      - "1 large onion, finely diced"
      - "2 tsp ginger-garlic paste"
      - "400g tomato puree"
      - "1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder"
      - "1 tsp garam masala"
      - "1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)"
      - "1 cup heavy cream"
      - "Salt and sugar to taste"

steps:
  - "Combine chicken with all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight for best flavor."
  - "Heat a large pan or grill over medium-high heat. Cook marinated chicken pieces until charred and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes per side. Set aside."
  - "In a large pan, heat butter and oil over medium heat. Add onions and cook until golden, about 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally."
  - "Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Add tomato puree, chili powder, and garam masala. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until the oil separates from the sauce."
  - "Stir in heavy cream and kasuri methi. Simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the cooked chicken pieces to the sauce. Simmer for 5-10 minutes until the chicken absorbs the flavors."
  - "Adjust salt and add a pinch of sugar to balance the acidity. Serve hot with naan or basmati rice."

tips:
  - "Marinating overnight makes a huge difference in flavor and tenderness"
  - "Kashmiri chili gives beautiful color without too much heat - don't substitute with regular chili powder"
  - "Crush the kasuri methi between your palms before adding to release its aroma"
